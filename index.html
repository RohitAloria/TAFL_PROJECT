The Subject

TAFL explores the mathematical foundations of computer science. It bridges abstract machines and real-world compilers, parsers, and programming languages — teaching us the power and limits of computation itself. From a simple DFA recognizing a string to a Turing machine deciding computability, every concept builds a rigorous mental model of how information can be processed, transformed, and constrained.
